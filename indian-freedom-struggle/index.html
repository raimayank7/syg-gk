Indian freedom struggle: GK questions and answers

From the Revolt of 1857 to the midnight of 15 August 1947, the freedom struggle is a story of movements, leaders and slogans. These questions follow that story in order, so read them as a timeline.

Test yourself

Ten questions picked at random from this topic, four choices each. Or go through every question as flash cards.

20 questions on Indian freedom struggle

  1. Which sepoy's action at Barrackpore in March 1857 is remembered as a spark of the Revolt? Mangal Pandey He served in the army of the East India Company.
  2. Which queen of Jhansi fought the British in 1857? Rani Lakshmibai She died fighting in 1858.
  3. Who was the last Mughal emperor, named leader of the Revolt of 1857? Bahadur Shah Zafar After the revolt the British sent him away to Rangoon.
  4. Which retired British officer took the lead in founding the Indian National Congress in 1885? A. O. Hume A. O. stands for Allan Octavian.
  5. Who was called the "Grand Old Man of India"? Dadabhai Naoroji He wrote about how wealth was being drained from India to Britain.
  6. In which year was Bengal partitioned by Lord Curzon? 1905 The partition was cancelled in 1911.
  7. Which movement grew out of the protest against the partition of Bengal? Swadeshi Movement People gave up foreign cloth and goods and used those made in India.
  8. In which year did Gandhiji return to India from South Africa? 1915 He returned on 9 January, now marked as Pravasi Bharatiya Divas.
  9. Where did Gandhiji lead his first satyagraha in India, in 1917? Champaran Champaran is in Bihar. He took up the cause of the indigo farmers.
  10. After which incident of 1922 did Gandhiji call off the Non-Cooperation Movement? Chauri Chaura A crowd set fire to a police station there, and Gandhiji would not accept violence.
  11. Who led the Bardoli Satyagraha of 1928? Vallabhbhai Patel The women of Bardoli gave him the title "Sardar".
  12. In which year did the Simon Commission come to India? 1928 It had no Indian member, so it was met with the cry "Simon, go back".
  13. Which leader, known as "Punjab Kesari", died after a lathi charge on a protest against the Simon Commission? Lala Lajpat Rai Punjab Kesari means "Lion of Punjab".
  14. Which revolutionary made the slogan "Inquilab Zindabad" famous? Bhagat Singh The slogan means "Long live the revolution". It was coined by Maulana Hasrat Mohani.
  15. At which session of the Congress, in 1929, was Purna Swaraj declared the goal? Lahore Jawaharlal Nehru presided over the session.
  16. Who was known as "Frontier Gandhi"? Khan Abdul Ghaffar Khan He was also called Badshah Khan.
  17. Who wrote the song "Sare Jahan Se Achha"? Muhammad Iqbal It was written in Urdu in 1904.
  18. Who was the last Viceroy of British India? Lord Mountbatten He stayed on as the first Governor-General of independent India.
  19. Who was the only Indian Governor-General of independent India? C. Rajagopalachari He was popularly called Rajaji.
  20. On which date did India become independent? 15 August 1947 The day is celebrated every year as Independence Day.

These are well-established facts, written in our own words and last checked on 11 November 2026. If your textbook says something different, follow your textbook and tell us.
