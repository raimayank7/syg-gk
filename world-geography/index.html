World geography: GK questions and answers

The biggest, the highest, the deepest: these are the record-holders of our planet, along with a few facts about maps and famous nicknames of countries.

Test yourself

Ten questions picked at random from this topic, four choices each. Or go through every question as flash cards.

20 questions on world geography

  1. Which is the largest continent? Asia Asia is also the continent with the most people.
  2. Which is the smallest continent? Australia Australia is at once a continent and a country.
  3. How many continents are there? Seven Asia, Africa, North America, South America, Antarctica, Europe and Australia.
  4. Which is the largest ocean? Pacific Ocean The Pacific covers about one third of the Earth's surface.
  5. Which is the highest mountain peak in the world? Mount Everest It is 8,848.86 metres high and lies in the Himalayas.
  6. Which is the largest country in the world by area? Russia Russia stretches across both Europe and Asia.
  7. Which is the smallest country in the world? Vatican City It lies inside the city of Rome and is headed by the Pope.
  8. Which is the largest hot desert in the world? Sahara It is in northern Africa.
  9. Which river carries the most water in the world? Amazon It flows through South America.
  10. Which is the largest island in the world? Greenland Australia is counted as a continent, not an island.
  11. The deepest known part of the oceans is in which trench? Mariana Trench It is in the Pacific Ocean.
  12. Which country is called the "Land of the Rising Sun"? Japan The Japanese name of the country, Nippon, means "origin of the sun".
  13. Which country is called the "Land of the Midnight Sun"? Norway In the far north of Norway the sun does not set for weeks in summer.
  14. The Great Barrier Reef lies off the coast of which country? Australia It is the largest coral reef system in the world.
  15. Which imaginary line divides the Earth into the Northern and Southern Hemispheres? Equator The Equator is the line of 0° latitude.
  16. The Prime Meridian (0° longitude) passes through which place? Greenwich Greenwich is in London.
  17. Which canal connects the Mediterranean Sea and the Red Sea? Suez Canal The canal is in Egypt and was opened in 1869.
  18. Which canal connects the Atlantic and Pacific Oceans? Panama Canal The canal cuts across Panama in Central America and was opened in 1914.
  19. Which is the longest mountain range on land? Andes It runs along the western edge of South America.
  20. Which is the coldest continent? Antarctica Almost all of it is covered by a thick sheet of ice.

These are well-established facts, written in our own words and last checked on 11 November 2026. If your textbook says something different, follow your textbook and tell us.
