How to use this site

  1. Choose a topic and read its questions and answers once.
  2. Tap "Hide answers" and say each answer before you tap to check it.
  3. Take the ten-question practice quiz. Repeat until you score ten out of ten.
  4. Come back tomorrow for the daily quiz, which mixes all topics.

Why this works, and how to remember what you learn, is explained in How to prepare general knowledge.

Common questions

Is this GK for a particular class or exam?

The questions are basic static GK, useful from about class 4 upwards, for school quizzes, olympiads, scholarship tests and as a foundation for competitive exams.

Do you cover current affairs?

No. This site covers facts that stay true. For current affairs, read a daily newspaper.

Are the answers reliable?

We use only well-established facts and leave out disputed ones. Each topic shows the date it was last checked. If you spot a mistake, tell us through the contact page.

Is my score saved?

Only in your own browser, to show your best score and daily streak. Nothing is sent to us.
